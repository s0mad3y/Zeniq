# Introducing ZENIQ

ZENIQ is a markerless motion capture application designed to extract **3D skeletal movement from ordinary webcam footage**.

Traditional motion capture systems can require expensive cameras, tracking suits, physical markers, and large recording spaces. ZENIQ takes a different approach.

## Motion capture using the cameras you already own

ZENIQ recognizes the skeletal structure of a real performer and synchronizes footage from multiple webcams simultaneously.

This gives creators access to multiple viewing angles without requiring dedicated motion capture hardware.

## Built for animation and virtual characters

Users can use ZENIQ to:

- Record performances for animation
- Export motion as BVH files
- Livestream virtual characters
- Transmit real-time motion data
- Enable or disable hand tracking
- Enable or disable face mesh tracking

## A simpler setup

The goal is to keep setup approachable.

Users launch ZENIQ, connect their available cameras, choose their tracking options, and begin recording.

No marker suit. No dedicated capture stage. No expensive sensor equipment.

## What comes next

The ZENIQ team is continuing to improve real-time multi-camera tracking, webcam compatibility, Unreal Engine integration, and export quality.

We want professional motion capture to become accessible to students, independent creators, VTubers, animators, and studios.
